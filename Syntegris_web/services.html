Our Technical Services

Specialized engineering support, software implementation, and specialized treatments.

Software Training & Implementation

We don't just supply software; we ensure your team is fully capable of using it. We provide structured training programs for SDC Verifier, DANTE, and Stampack Express, tailored to your specific industry applications.

Our implementation services include setting up simulation environments, creating custom verification reports, and automating workflow processes to improve engineering efficiency.

PIT Treatment Services

Pneumatic Impact Treatment (PIT) is a specialized mechanical treatment used to improve the fatigue life of welded components. We provide on-site PIT services to reduce residual stresses and enhance the durability of critical infrastructure and machinery.

FEA Simulation Support

Our team provides technical support for Finite Element Analysis (FEA). We assist in mesh generation, boundary condition setup, and post-processing results to ensure your simulations accurately reflect real-world physics.

WAAM Technical Support

We support the technical execution of Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM) projects. This includes path planning optimization, deposition strategy, and metallurgical testing to ensure 3D-printed metal parts meet industrial quality standards.
