SDC Verifier

Engineering Verification According to Industry Standards

Streamlined Structural Verification

SDC Verifier is a powerful post-processor for FEA software (like Ansys, Simcenter, and Femap) that automates the verification process according to various industry standards such as Eurocode, DIN, AISC, and ISO.

It is specifically designed for engineers working on cranes, lifting equipment, offshore structures, and maritime engineering where safety and compliance are mandatory.

Key Features

  • Automatic Beam and Plate Buckling checks.
  • Fatigue calculations for welded structures.
  • Comprehensive reporting tools that save up to 70% of engineering time.
  • Joint and Weld checks according to international standards.
