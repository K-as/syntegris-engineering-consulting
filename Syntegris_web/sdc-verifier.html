SDC Verifier

The complete standard verification solution for Ansys, Femap, and Simcenter 3D.

Build with Confidence, Validate with Ease

SDC Verifier transforms the tedious process of manual code checking into an automated, precise workflow. It empowers structural engineers to verify designs against 50+ international codes instantly.

What You’ll Achieve with SDC Verifier

A unified platform for calculation, verification, and reporting.

Comprehensive Design Checks

Perform complex bolt, fatigue, buckling, and strength checks for diverse project requirements in a single interface.

Effortless Standards Verification

Validate models against 50+ codes (Eurocode, AISC, API, DNV) with automated processes that save hours of manual cross-referencing.

Time-Saving Automation

Reduce engineering effort by up to 80% with automated tasks for structural recognition, calculation, and full report generation.

FEA Analysis & Simulations

Leverage the industry-standard Nastran solver for accurate simulations and failure predictions directly within your workflow.

CAD-to-FEA Verification

Verify designs using the same CAD-based FEA model—no re-modelling required. Ensure consistency between design and analysis.

Proven Results

Generate calculation reports that are trusted by major certification bodies (DNV, Bureau Veritas) and leading engineering teams worldwide.
