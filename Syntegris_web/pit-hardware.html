PIT Equipment

High Frequency Mechanical Impact (HFMI) Technology for Weld Life Extension

What is Pneumatic Impact Treatment?

Pneumatic Impact Treatment (PIT) is a cutting-edge High Frequency Mechanical Impact (HFMI) method designed to significantly increase the fatigue life of welded structures. By applying high-frequency impulses to the weld toe, PIT modifies the residual stress profile from tensile to compressive.

This compact, handheld hardware is essential for industries dealing with cyclic loading, such as cranes, bridges, offshore structures, and mining equipment.

Fatigue Life Extension

PIT treatment can extend the fatigue life of welded joints by 5x to 10x compared to untreated welds. It effectively "resets" the fatigue clock on aging structures.

Stress Relief

Welding creates harmful tensile residual stresses. PIT introduces beneficial compressive stresses into the material surface, preventing crack initiation and propagation.

The PIT System

Portable & Powerful

The PIT system consists of a control unit and a handheld impact tool. It is designed for mobility, allowing engineers to perform treatments directly on-site, even in difficult-to-reach areas.

  • Operating Frequency: ~90 Hz
  • Power Supply: Standard Compressed Air (6 bar)
  • Application Speed: Approx. 30 cm/min
  • Pin Radius: Optimized for weld geometry (2mm - 4mm)
