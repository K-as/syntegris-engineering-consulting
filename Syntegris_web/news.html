January 7, 2026

PIT Equipment Training at UiTM

We successfully conducted a specialized training session on Pneumatic Impact Treatment (PIT) equipment at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM). We were honored to invite Sara Solutions Sdn Bhd to participate in this technical workshop.

The session covered the fundamentals of High Frequency Mechanical Impact (HFMI) for extending the fatigue life of welded structures.

Hands-On Demonstration:
Beyond theory, the session provided a valuable opportunity for participants to try out the PIT equipment themselves. They received direct guidance on how to operate the tool, allowing them to experience the treatment process firsthand and understand the application mechanics in a real-world setting.
