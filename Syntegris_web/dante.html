DANTE Solutions

The industry standard for heat treatment simulation. Improve part performance and reduce scrap by predicting distortion, residual stress, and metallurgical phases before production.

DANTE FEA Solver

The core engine of the DANTE system. This Finite Element Analysis (FEA) solver links thermal, mechanical, and metallurgical models to simulate the entire heat treatment cycle.

  • Accurate Distortion Prediction Minimize machining allowance and optimize fixture design.
  • Residual Stress Analysis Identify cracking risks and improve fatigue life performance.
  • Phase Transformation Track Austenite, Ferrite, Pearlite, Bainite, and Martensite formation.

Process Design & Optimization

A suite of specialized utilities to define and optimize your heating and carburizing schedules.

  • VCarb (Vacuum Carburizing) Simulate boost/diffuse schedules to achieve exact carbon profiles.
  • GCarb (Gas Carburizing) Model atmosphere potential interaction with steel surfaces.
  • GNitro (Gas Nitriding) Predict nitrogen depth and white layer formation.
  • HTP Sim General Heat Treatment Process Simulator for heating/cooling cycles.

Material Characterization

Tools to generate material models and fit experimental data for custom alloys.

Prediction Tools

  • TTT Gen Generate TTT diagrams.
  • CHT Gen Continuous heating models.
  • Jominy Predictor Hardenability curves.
  • Mat Sim Phase visualization.

Data Fitting

  • Dilot Fit Fit dilatometry data.
  • Mec Fit Fit mechanical stress-strain data.
