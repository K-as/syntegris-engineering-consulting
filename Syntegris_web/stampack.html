Stampack Xpress

Sheet Metal Forming Simulation

Stampack Xpress is a practical simulation tool designed for toolmakers and method planners. It validates the forming process of sheet metal parts quickly and accurately, without requiring deep FEA expertise.

  • Cracks & Thinning: Detect material failure early.
  • Wrinkling Analysis: Visualize compression zones.
  • Springback Compensation: Accurate prediction of material elastic recovery.
  • Material Savings: Optimize blank sizes to reduce scrap and cost.
  • Fast Turnaround: Easy-to-use interface designed for shop-floor engineers.
