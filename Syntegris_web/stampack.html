Virtual Try-Out for Tool Designers

Validate your sheet metal forming process virtually. Identify splits, wrinkles, and springback before you cut a single piece of steel.

Why Stampack Xpress?

The Old Way (Trial & Error)

Building the physical tool, testing it on the press, finding cracks, grinding the tool, and testing again. This is expensive and delays delivery.

The Stampack Way

Simulate the process in minutes. Stampack Xpress is designed for toolmakers, not scientists. No deep FEA knowledge is required to get accurate results.

Simulation Modules

Tailored solutions for every sheet metal thickness and process type.

  • Shell Simulation Ideal for thin sheets and rapid prototyping. Fast calculation speed for quick iterations.
  • Solid Simulation Essential for thick plates, coining, and ironing. Calculates stress through the thickness.
  • Springback Compensation Automatically modifies tool geometry to counteract material bounce-back.

Detect Defects Early

Secure your process reliability and reduce scrap rates.

  • Material Failure (Cracks) Identify zones where thinning exceeds limits or material splits occur.
  • Wrinkling Analysis Visualize compression zones to prevent visible surface defects.
  • Trim Optimization Calculate the optimal blank outline to ensure the final part net shape is correct.
