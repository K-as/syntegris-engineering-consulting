Engineering Consultancy

Structural Integrity & Verification

Our core expertise lies in verifying complex structures against international codes such as Eurocode 3, AISC, DNV, and API. We help clients ensure their designs are safe, compliant, and optimized for weight and cost.

FEA & Simulation

We provide advanced Finite Element Analysis (FEA) services, ranging from static stress analysis to dynamic fatigue assessment. Our team uses industry-leading software to simulate real-world conditions.

Heat Treatment Strategy

Consulting on welding and heat treatment processes using DANTE simulation. We assist in predicting hardness, residual stress, and distortion to optimize your manufacturing cycle before the first part is even produced.

Manufacturing & WAAM Advisory

With deep expertise in Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM) and smart manufacturing, we provide strategic roadmaps for companies looking to integrate 3D metal printing into their production lines.

Our advisory includes material selection, process parameter optimization, and metallurgical considerations for high-performance components.
