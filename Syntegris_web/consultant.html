PIT Treatment consultation

Pneumatic Impact Treatment (PIT) is a specialized mechanical treatment used to improve the fatigue life of welded components. We provide on-site PIT services to reduce residual stresses and enhance the durability of critical infrastructure.

  • Extend fatigue life by up to 10x
  • On-site & offshore application
  • Weld toe stress relief
